Terms and Conditions of System Operation

Law Enforcement Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Reference: CP-TERMS-2026-A | Official Department Order

1. Authorization and Permitted Personnel

Access to this terminal is restricted to credentialed police officers verified against the official departmental registry. Self-registration, guest credentials, or unauthorized access attempts are prohibited and are subject to immediate audit review and disciplinary action under police conduct regulations.

2. Emergency Clearance Dispatch Protocol

The emergency alert function must only be engaged upon positive visual or radio confirmation of an emergency medical vehicle in transit. Engaging an alert for non-emergency or testing purposes outside of designated drill periods is strictly forbidden.

3. Duty Session and Telemetry Integrity

Officers on active post must keep their mobile dispatch device active and location services enabled to ensure accurate forward-cone calculation. Officers must formally select "END DUTY" when relieved of duty or when departing assigned traffic sectors.

4. Rate Limiting and Misuse Prevention

Automated rate limiters are active on all dispatch endpoints. Any attempt to bypass security filters, flood WebSocket queues, or manipulate spatial telemetry will result in immediate credential revocation.

5. System Modifications and Compliance

The department reserves the right to modify system protocols, alert thresholds, and security parameters in alignment with updated emergency traffic guidelines.
