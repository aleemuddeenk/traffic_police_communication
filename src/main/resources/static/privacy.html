Police Department Telemetry Privacy Policy

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Reference: CP-SEC-2026-POL | Effective Date: October 2026

1. Operational Purpose

ClearPath is an authorized internal government dispatch network designed exclusively for verified traffic police officers. Its sole function is the coordination of emergency vehicle route clearance along active corridors. This service is restricted to verified law enforcement personnel.

2. Telemetry and Location Data Collection

Location coordinates (latitude, longitude, heading, and movement speed) are captured strictly while an officer maintains an active "ON DUTY" status. Telemetry collection terminates immediately when the officer selects "END DUTY" or when a session expires. Offline or off-duty location tracking is strictly prohibited by system architecture.

3. Data Access and Retention Policy

All location pings, alert transmissions, and officer acknowledgments are recorded in a secured audit log for chain-of-custody compliance. Telemetry records are retained in compliance with jurisdictional law enforcement data retention mandates and are accessible solely to authorized division dispatch supervisors.

4. Security and Cryptographic Standards

Authentication tokens are signed using HMAC-SHA256 with 24-hour expiration intervals. Passwords are encrypted using salted BCrypt hashing. Network transport across client devices and dispatch brokers is enforced via TLS encryption.

5. Department Contact

Inquiries regarding this policy or system auditing may be directed to the Traffic Police Dispatch Communications Division, Information Security Unit.
